# Limitations and Responsible Use

- FAERS signals are not evidence of causality.
- Spontaneous reporting is affected by under-reporting, stimulated reporting, indication bias, co-medication, and missing exposure denominators.
- Keyword immune-AE detection is a screening heuristic, not a validated MedDRA mapping.
- Tox21 endpoint labels may be sparse and missing-not-at-random.
- Scaffold validation can be conservative and does not replace external validation.
- SHAP explains model associations, not mechanisms.
- Risk-engine weights are illustrative until validated by toxicology, pharmacovigilance, clinical-safety, and medicinal-chemistry experts.
- Prospective experimental validation is required before operational use.
